
Why Llama 3.1 8B – Key Technical Pointers


Efficient inference footprint-Lower hardware and VRAM requirements enable single-GPU inference with better cost–performance efficiency.
url-https://huggingface.co/blog/llama31 (evidence)
High throughput performance- Faster token generation and higher tokens-per-second per GPU compared to larger models.

Intent-focused reasoning- Aligns generated questions and criteria to control and sub-control intent, not surface wording.

Strong instruction adherence - Maintains defined question type, scope boundaries, and logical structure consistently.

Hierarchical context retention - Preserves control and sub-control relationships across long-context inputs.

Controlled and bounded generation - Produces structured, well-scoped, and well-bounded questions and criteria.

Output stability - Delivers predictable, low-variance outputs across runs, reducing inconsistency.

Consistency over peak scoring - Selection favors stable semantic behavior across thresholds rather than isolated peak scores.

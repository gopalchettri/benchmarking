Architectural Definition of RAG-Based Policy Chatbot
________________________________________
Policy Chatbot (Logical Architecture)
The Policy Chatbot serves as the interaction layer between users and enterprise policy knowledge. User queries are received through the application interface and routed to backend services for processing. The chatbot itself does not store or interpret policy content; it orchestrates query handling by invoking retrieval and inference components. This separation ensures responses are always driven by authoritative backend knowledge sources.
________________________________________
Knowledge Management Architecture
Policy documents are prepared through a centralized knowledge management architecture. Documents are ingested from source repositories, parsed to extract text, and processed to preserve logical structure such as sections and clauses. The processed content is organized into context-preserving units, ensuring consistency, traceability, and readiness for retrieval without performing inference at this stage.
________________________________________
Knowledge Base & RAG Processing
Prepared policy units are converted into vector embeddings using embedding models and stored in a centralized vector-based knowledge base. This knowledge base forms the cornerstone of retrieval-augmented generation by enabling semantic similarity search. When a query is received, the input is embedded and matched against stored vectors to retrieve the most relevant and contextually aligned policy information.
________________________________________
Inference & LLM Interaction
The retrieved policy context is combined with the user query and passed to the language model during inference. The language model reasons strictly over the provided context to generate responses grounded in enterprise policy documents. This interaction ensures outputs are accurate, context-aware, and aligned with organizational standards, with optional validation or formatting applied before returning the final response.
